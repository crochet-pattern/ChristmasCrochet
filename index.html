Start your Christmas crochet early

  • Most holiday projects are small and use very little yarn.
  • Red, green and white make every project look cohesive.
  • Use cotton yarn for ornaments so they keep their shape.
  • Embroider faces on toys for small children.
  • Add the hanging loop while you work, not after.
  • Keep crochet decorations away from candles and flames.
